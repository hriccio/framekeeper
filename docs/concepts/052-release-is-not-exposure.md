# Release Is Not Exposure

Release and exposure are different steps.

## Meaning

Release is the internal decision that the current version is acceptable. Exposure
is the operational act of putting that accepted state into contact with a real
context.

## Related Episodes

- [Release Decisions Stay Explicit](../episodes/050-release-decisions-stay-explicit.md)
- [Exposure Follows Acceptance](../episodes/051-exposure-follows-acceptance.md)

## Notes

- keep the decision distinct from the operational step
- do not merge acceptance with exposure
- expose only after release is accepted
