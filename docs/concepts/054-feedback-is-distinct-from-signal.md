# Feedback Is Distinct From Signal

Feedback and signal are not the same thing.

## Meaning

Signal is internal, diagnostic orientation before release. Feedback is evidence
captured after exposure to a real context.

## Notes

- keep signal internal
- keep feedback post-exposure
- do not merge review diagnostics with lived evidence
