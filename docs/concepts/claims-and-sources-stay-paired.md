# Claims And Sources Stay Paired

Claims are easier to trust when the source stays nearby.

## Meaning

A claim without a source invites doubt. A source without a claim invites
confusion. The two should travel together when the page needs evidence.

## Notes

- keep evidence close to the point being made
- do not scatter source context across the page
- make the relationship between claim and source easy to read
