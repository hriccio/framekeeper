# Curated References Are Evidence

References are evidence for why a page says what it says.

## Meaning

They are not filler and not a bibliography dump. They help justify the content
layer by showing where the supporting context comes from.

## Notes

- keep curation deliberate
- prefer references that add context or verify a claim
- avoid references that only repeat the same point
