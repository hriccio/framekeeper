# Draft Candidates Need A Destination

Draft candidates are not meant to remain indefinite placeholders.

## Meaning

Once an idea is extracted, it should be steered toward a destination that fits
its purpose. That destination keeps the page useful and the work tractable.

## Notes

- decide the page family early
- keep the destination visible in the draft
- avoid leaving candidate material directionless
