# Drafts Are Not Final

Drafts are intermediate work, not the published knowledge layer.

## Meaning

This boundary keeps the site honest about what is ready for readers and what is
still being shaped.

- [Drafts Become Refined Pages](../episodes/010-drafts-become-refined-pages.md)

## Notes

- draft state should be visible
- final content should be explicit
- do not mix the two in one page without clear labeling
