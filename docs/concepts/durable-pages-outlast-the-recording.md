# Durable Pages Outlast The Recording

Durable pages should preserve the idea after the original recording is gone.

## Meaning

A public page needs to stand on its own. It can point back to the recording, but
it should not depend on the recording to remain useful.

## Notes

- preserve the idea, not the moment
- make the page understandable without the full video
- keep the recording as a source, not the form
