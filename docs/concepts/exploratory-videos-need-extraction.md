# Exploratory Videos Need Extraction

Exploratory recordings do not turn into pages by themselves.

## Meaning

The video is conversational and in motion. Extraction is what turns the moving
material into reusable page candidates.

- keep the recording as source material
- extract the reusable idea
- do not expect raw speech to stay durable on its own
