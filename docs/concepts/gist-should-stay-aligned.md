# Gist Should Stay Aligned

The gist in the title and summary should stay aligned with the actual page.

## Meaning

If the preview and the content diverge, the page becomes harder to trust. The
reader should be able to move from the short form to the full page without
feeling misled.

- keep the preview faithful
- avoid hype or bait
- preserve the relationship between the short form and the page itself
