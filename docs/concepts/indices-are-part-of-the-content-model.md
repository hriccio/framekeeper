# Indices Are Part Of The Content Model

Indices are not just navigation aids. They are part of the maintained content
surface.

## Meaning

If the indices are stale, the site is harder to trust. Keeping them current is
part of keeping the content model honest.

- [Metadata Keeps Pages Readable](../episodes/012-metadata-keeps-pages-readable.md)

## Notes

- keep the index entries current
- treat the indices as visible parts of the site
- do not let index maintenance become an afterthought
