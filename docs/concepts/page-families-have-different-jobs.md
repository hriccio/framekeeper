# Page Families Have Different Jobs

The page families in the knowledge layer are intentionally different.

## Meaning

Episodes, concepts, references, and notes each exist for a distinct editorial
purpose. Keeping that distinction visible makes the site easier to understand
and easier to extend.

## Notes

- do not treat every page as the same kind of artifact
- keep the family roles readable from the page layout
- avoid collapsing the site into one generic content bucket
