# Page Shape Should Stay Predictable

The structure of a page should stay predictable enough that a reader can scan
it quickly.

## Meaning

Predictable shape makes the site easier to browse, easier to edit, and easier
to review after the fact.

## Notes

- keep the page format stable
- preserve the same high-level sections across similar pages
- avoid introducing arbitrary structural variation
