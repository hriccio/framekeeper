# Public Content Needs Selection

Public content should be chosen, not merely exported.

## Meaning

Selection is part of editorial work. The public site needs pages that have been
curated for usefulness, clarity, and context.

## Related Episodes

- [Raw Transcripts Stay Private](../episodes/026-raw-transcripts-stay-private.md)
- [Public Pages Are Refined](../episodes/027-public-pages-are-refined.md)

## Notes

- choose what helps the reader
- keep only the material that belongs on the site
- avoid publishing every intermediate artifact
