# Raw Evidence Is Not The Site

Raw inputs are not the same thing as public knowledge pages.

## Meaning

Evidence can inform the site without becoming the site. The published layer
should stay focused on the refined result.

## Related Episodes

- [Raw Transcripts Stay Private](../episodes/026-raw-transcripts-stay-private.md)
- [Public Pages Are Refined](../episodes/027-public-pages-are-refined.md)

## Notes

- keep raw material in the working layer
- publish the refined artifact, not the scratchpad
- preserve the boundary between source and presentation
