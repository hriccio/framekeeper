# WNT And CodingZen Stay Separate

The knowledge layer is its own site and should not be folded into other
projects by default.

## Meaning

WastingNoTime stays more curated and structurally clean. CodingZen stays more
focused on exploration, creative coding, visualization, and play. This
knowledge layer keeps a different shape because it tracks exploratory channel
content.
