## Core idea

Automation should handle the mechanical parts of the workflow, not the meaning
of the content.

---

## What I said

The useful part of the system is not only that it can move files around or
generate outputs. The point is to reduce friction on the mechanical side while
keeping interpretation, judgment, and publication authority with the human.

---

## Key points

- mechanics can be automated
- meaning should not be automated away
- the workflow stays useful when it remains easy to inspect
