## Core idea

Only the safety stage can block the workflow.

---

## What I said

If the content is risky, the pipeline should stop at the safety gate. Signal
feedback may still be useful, but it must remain diagnostic rather than
authoritative.

---

## Key points

- safety is a hard gate
- signal is not a gate
- policy risk and quality are different concerns
