## Core idea

References support episodes instead of replacing them.

---

## What I said

An episode can point to sources, policy pages, and supporting ideas, but the
episode itself remains the place where the reasoning is organized into a
channel-specific narrative.

---

## Key points

- episodes are the main content layer
- references support claims and context
- the site should stay readable even when the references are removed
