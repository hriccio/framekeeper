## Core idea

Source links keep the site honest by preserving where the ideas came from.

---

## What I said

If a page makes a claim, the supporting source should be easy to find. That
keeps the knowledge layer inspectable and makes it easier to revisit decisions
later.

---

## Key points

- provenance matters
- published content should not obscure its origin
- raw evidence should remain outside the published site by default
