## Core idea

Concept pages should point back to the episodes that motivated them.

---

## What I said

Concepts are reusable, but they still come from specific thinking in specific
videos. The links back to episodes keep the concept pages honest about where the
idea came from.

---

## Key points

- concepts need episode context
- reuse should not erase origin
- the knowledge layer should stay inspectable
