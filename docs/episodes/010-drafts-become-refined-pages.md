# Drafts Become Refined Pages

## Metadata

- YouTube channel: `@umoutrohenrique`
- Status: refined

---

## Core idea

Draft content becomes refined pages when the structure, links, and intent are
clear enough to publish.

---

## What I said

A draft is useful while ideas are still moving. A refined page is what remains
after the idea has settled enough to be shared in the knowledge layer.

---

## Key points

- drafts are intermediate
- refined pages are explicit and readable
- the transition should stay visible
