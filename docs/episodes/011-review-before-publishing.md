## Metadata

- YouTube channel: `@umoutrohenrique`

## Core idea

Review should happen before publishing so the final page preserves intent and
context.

---

## What I said

The review step is where the content gets checked for clarity, structure, and
fit. It is not an excuse to add hidden gates or make publication opaque.

---

## Key points

- review preserves context
- publishing remains a human decision
- the page should stay focused on the review step
