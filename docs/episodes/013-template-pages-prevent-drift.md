## Metadata

- YouTube channel: `@umoutrohenrique`

## Core idea

Templates keep the pages from drifting apart as the site grows.

---

## What I said

The template is not there to remove judgment. It is there so every page keeps a
familiar structure and the site remains easy to navigate.

---

## Key points

- templates support consistency
- consistency supports maintenance
- templates should not flatten editorial differences
