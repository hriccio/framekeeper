## Core idea

Older pages still matter because they show how the site thinking changed over
time.

---

## What I said

The site should not pretend that only the newest pages are valid. Older pages
help readers see the line of thought that produced the current shape of the
knowledge layer.

---

## Key points

- older pages remain part of the record
- updates should not hide prior thinking
- archived pages still contribute context
