## Core idea

Summaries should preserve the gist without trying to replace the page.

---

## What I said

A summary is useful when it helps the reader get the point quickly and then
decide whether to read the full page. It should stay faithful to the actual
content.

---

## Key points

- summaries should be faithful
- summaries should help scanning
- summaries are secondary to the page itself
