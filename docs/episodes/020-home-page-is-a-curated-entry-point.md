# Home Page Is A Curated Entry Point

## Metadata

- YouTube channel: `@umoutrohenrique`
- Status: refined

---

## Core idea

The home page should act as a curated entry point, not a dump of every page.

---

## What I said

The front door of the site should make it easy to understand what matters most
right now. It should point to the pages that best represent the knowledge layer
without trying to surface everything equally.

---

## Key points

- the home page is selective
- curated links help readers orient quickly
- the front door should not turn into a sitemap
