## Metadata

## Core idea

References work better when they stay targeted to the idea being explained.

---

## What I said

Not every useful link belongs on every page. A good reference points to the
source that actually helps the reader understand the claim being made.

---

## Key points

- references should be selected, not accumulated
- the page should support the claim, not distract from it
- a reference list can become noise when it grows without purpose
