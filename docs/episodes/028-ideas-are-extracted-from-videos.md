## Metadata

- YouTube channel: `@umoutrohenrique`

## Core idea

Videos become useful for the knowledge layer when ideas are extracted from
them.

---

## What I said

The transcript is a starting point. The useful part is the idea that can be
carried into a durable page.

---

## Key points

- extraction is selective
- a video can produce more than one candidate idea
- the knowledge layer should preserve the idea, not the full recording
