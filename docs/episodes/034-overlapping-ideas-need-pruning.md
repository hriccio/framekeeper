# Overlapping Ideas Need Pruning

## Metadata

- YouTube channel: `@umoutrohenrique`
- Status: refined

---

## Core idea

When ideas overlap too much, the site should prune the excess and keep the
distinct version.

---

## What I said

The knowledge layer gets harder to use when the same idea appears in too many
places with too little difference.

---

## Key points

- overlap creates noise
- pruning preserves the clearest version
- the page family should remain easy to scan
