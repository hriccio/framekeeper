## Core idea

Pages read better when unnecessary overlap has been trimmed away.

---

## What I said

A tighter page is easier to trust and easier to navigate. The reader should not
have to compare nearly identical pages to recover the point.

---

## Key points

- pruning helps readability
- repeated material should not sprawl
- the page should stay focused on one job
