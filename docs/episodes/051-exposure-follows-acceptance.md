## Core idea

Exposure happens after the release decision has been accepted.

## What I said

Once a state is accepted, it can be put into contact with a real context. That
is a separate step from deciding the state is ready.

## Key points

- acceptance comes first
- exposure is operational
- the released state needs a place and a form
