# Published Content Is Not Raw Evidence

## Purpose

This note records the boundary between raw evidence and published knowledge
layer content.

## Notes

- raw evidence belongs under `work/sources/`
- published content belongs under `docs/`
- the knowledge layer can describe the workflow without absorbing raw material
- intermediate notes are not a place to stash raw transcript blobs
