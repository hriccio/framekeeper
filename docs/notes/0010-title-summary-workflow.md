# Title Summary Workflow

## Purpose

This note records how titles and summaries are kept in sync.

## Notes

- title and summary should be revised together
- both should stay faithful to the page content
- the preview should help the reader rather than pressure the content
- the workflow should remain simple enough to repeat manually
