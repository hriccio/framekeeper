# Candidate Promotion Workflow

## Purpose

This note records how a candidate idea moves into a page family.

## Notes

- identify the candidate idea from the transcript
- decide whether it belongs in episodes, concepts, references, or notes
- shape the draft for that family
- refine the page until it fits the target family
