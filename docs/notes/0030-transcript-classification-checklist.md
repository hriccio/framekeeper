# Transcript Classification Checklist

## Purpose

This note records a simple checklist for classifying transcript-derived ideas
into the right page family.

## Notes

- identify the main angle of the idea
- decide whether the source is the point
- decide whether the idea explains a principle or tells a story
- choose the page family that matches the idea's main job
